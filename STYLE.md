# phasor style rubric

Your taste, in your words. The art-director pass (Milestone 7) critiques storyboards
against this file and `gallery/`. Fill it in; until then the critic has nothing to judge by.

## Hierarchy
_One focal point per beat. What else?_

## Negative space
_How much empty frame feels right?_

## Color restraint
_How many hues, which ones carry meaning?_

## Motion rhythm
_What should never move at the same time?_

## Typography
_Sizes, weights, when to use math vs text._
