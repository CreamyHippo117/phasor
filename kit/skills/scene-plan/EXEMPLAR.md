# The exemplar: the opening of reverb A1

This is the one fixed point every plan is held against. Keep it exactly. When a rule in
`SKILL.md` or `CASEBOOK.md` seems to argue against what this opening did, the opening wins

## What it is

The first two minutes of the person's plan "reverb A1" (2026-11-27). A clap in a room. The
sound leaves the clap, reaches the walls and comes back. Then the room unfolds: each wall
becomes a mirror and the room behind it holds its own clap. The mirror rooms tile outward
into a crystal of rooms, each holding a copy of the clap, farther away and fainter the
farther out it sits. The viewer is given room to watch it, with the music forward.

(Described from the person's account. When the plan's text is in `plans/`, put its
opening here word for word and keep the description beside it.)

## Why it worked

The person's words (2026-11-28): it was the exemplar "because it established LTI, a key
concept needed to explain reverb in the context of ODEs, linear algebra and FFTs, while
being literally beautiful." That sentence has four parts, and a segment that matches
this one has all four.

1. **It is load-bearing.** It establishes the concept the rest of the video stands on.
   Each mirror room is one echo, so what reaches the listener is a sum of delayed, scaled
   copies of the clap: h(t) = Σ a_k δ(t − τ_k). Scaled copies that add is linearity. A
   later clap makes the same crystal later, which is time invariance. Convolution, the room
   as a matrix, its eigenvectors and the FFT are all built on that. Without the opening,
   every later step would rest on an assertion.
2. **The picture is the derivation.** The method of images *is* the unfolding. The sum is
   read off the crystal, one room at a time, so there was nothing to lay beside the
   picture. The math was enacted, not placed.
3. **It is watched.** It is beautiful in itself, with the sound off, and it is given the
   time to be looked at. Nothing crowds it.
4. **It stays on the question.** It is about why a room sounds the way it does from its
   first second. The beauty does not wander off to a picture the video never needs again.

The four come together. It is not a balance of beauty against math. The beauty went into
the foundation.

## The questions to ask of every segment

- What later step stands on this? If nothing does, it is a detour, however beautiful or
  true it is.
- Is its math enacted by the picture, or laid beside it?
- Would it be watched with the sound off, and is it given the time?
- Does it answer, or move toward, the video's opening question?
- Held next to the A1 opening, is it as true, as load-bearing and as watched? If not,
  which of the four is missing?
