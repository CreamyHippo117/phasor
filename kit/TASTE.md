# Taste

What the person likes and dislikes, kept as fixed points that accumulate, oldest first.
Read it before every scene; it outranks the examples.

Each entry is dated, keeps the person's own words, and is tagged with its kind:

- **Keep**: something that worked and must survive every later change.
- **Change**: something that failed, and what to do instead.

An entry is never deleted, never reworded and never "replaces" another. When a later entry
seems to conflict with an earlier one, it narrows it: a note under the earlier entry says
what still holds and what no longer does, and both stay. A reaction to the last output
tends to overshoot, so a Change says what went wrong, not the opposite of the last rule.
